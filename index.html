HOW PICTURES ARE SHARED

Pictures stay with the presentation

The recommended method is to upload picture files rather than rely on picture links. Uploaded pictures are stored inside the presentation that pupils download. An Educator can open the attached file in a web browser and project it without an account.

Online picture links can also be added, but may need internet access when presented. Ask pupils to include a caption and credit the original creator where possible. Educator email buttons are on the final screen.
